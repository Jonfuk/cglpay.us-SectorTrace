      Every figure here comes from a public-domain source and carries its own source URL and retrieval timestamp. Nothing is inferred: a value that could not be parsed is empty with a logged reason, not guessed.

      Read the caveats before quoting anything. They lead with the things that must not be computed from this data.

      Every figure on this site is read from a documented read-only API: the routes, their parameters and their response shapes are at /api. Each section's Download CSV carries every row its filters match, with the provenance and the row count written into the file.

      Most of this material is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0, which requires attribution. It is not all of it: the workforce census is NHS Benchmarking Network content with its own terms, and council documents vary by council. Every figure names its own licence in the Where this came from drawer beside it, and every export writes it into the file.
